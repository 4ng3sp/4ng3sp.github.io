I'm a master's student in statistics, with an undergraduate thesis in demography focused on migration in Italy between 1981 and 2021.

My undergraduate background is in Political Science at the University of Naples Federico II. My academic projects bring together several areas of applied analysis: customer churn and retention, football modelling, demographic research, and the exploration of networks and text.

This portfolio presents the questions behind those projects, the methods used to investigate them, and the limits that matter when interpreting the findings.
